# Psalms Way privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026. This policy covers the Psalms Way Android app and the
Psalms Way browser extension.

## In short

Psalms Way works without an account and keeps everything you save on your own
device. If you choose to sync, your data goes to a private folder in **your
own** Google Drive. We, the developer, never receive, see or store your data.
There is no Psalms Way server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What Psalms Way keeps on your device

Your bookmarks, notes, highlights, favourite chapters, reading history, streak,
badges, challenge progress and settings. They stay on your phone (Android app)
or in your browser's own storage (extension). All Bible text is built into the
apps; reading never needs the internet.

## Optional sync and backup with Google Drive

If you connect a Google account (in the app's **Cloud backup** screen or the
extension's **Sync** panel):

- **What is sent:** the items listed above, as one file.
- **Where it goes:** a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Psalms Way
  asks only for Google's `drive.appdata` permission, which lets it see that
  folder and nothing else in your Drive: not your documents, photos or other
  files.
- **Who receives it:** only Google, as your storage provider. The transfer goes
  straight from your device to Google over an encrypted connection.
- **Your email address** is read from Google so the app can show which account
  is connected. It stays on your device.
- **Sign-in tokens** stay on your device (in the browser, only in memory until
  the browser closes) and are never shared.

Psalms Way's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to keep your own
devices in step; it is never sold, never used for advertising and never
transferred to anyone.

## Your choices

- **Don't connect:** everything stays on your device.
- **Disconnect** at any time in Cloud backup (app) or Sync (extension). Your
  data stays on the device and in your Drive.
- **Delete the Drive copy:** Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Psalms Way →
  Delete hidden app data, or remove Psalms Way's access at
  [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
- **Delete everything on a device:** uninstall the app or remove the extension.

## Permissions

- **Android app:** notifications and vibration (only for the daily reminders you
  turn on) and internet (only for the optional Google Drive sync).
- **Browser extension:** `storage` (to keep your data in the browser) and, only
  when you connect sync, `identity` (Google sign-in). It cannot read or change
  any web page you visit.

## Children

Psalms Way collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
